# How self-haptics works

<FocusList>

- Track the user’s hands
- Position one hand as a physical prop
- Retarget the virtual hand
- Uses visual dominance
- Simulating virtual object contact through **self-contact**

</FocusList>

# Challenges and future possibilities

- Cannot reproduce every material, shape, or texture
- Works best for short, fast, impact-based interactions
- Hand overlap can cause tracking errors
- Better tracking and visual representation may improve realism

# Conclusion

::right::

## The **process** of self-haptics

Track → Position → Retarget → Contact

## The **effects** produced by self-haptics

Improve immersion and realism

## The **limitations** of self-haptics

Best suited for short, impact-based interactions
